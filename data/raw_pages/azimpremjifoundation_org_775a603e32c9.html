Azim Premji Scholarship for Girl Students The Azim Premji Foundation announces applications for scholarship programme supporting eligible girl students pursuing undergraduate education. Benefit: Financial support of ₹30,000 per year for regular undergraduate degree/diploma courses. Eligibility: Eligible girl students who have completed Class 10 and 12 from regular government schools and admitted to 1st year UG course. Gender: Eligible for female/girl students only. Application window open till: 30-11-2026.
