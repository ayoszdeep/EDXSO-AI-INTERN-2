Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship

The Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship aims to support meritorious students in pursuing their undergraduate studies in India.

Scholarship Benefit

Grant of ₹50,000 per annum for the complete duration of the degree programme.

Eligibility Criteria

Indian resident citizen who passed Class 12 with at least 60% marks and enrolled in the first year of a regular full-time undergraduate degree course.

Annual household income must be below Rs. 15 lakh.

Important Dates

Scheme Open from: 01-06-2026

Student application open till: 31-08-2026

Selection Process

Mandatory aptitude test and academic performance evaluation.
